### What we’re building
Inkwell is an AI-native writing platform. Its thesis is simple: a screenplay or document should be **structured data**, not a dead page. That unlocks:

- **Version control for writers** — save points (immutable snapshots), draft lines (parallel versions), and semantic changes you can actually read
- **Reviewable AI** — skills propose diffs; nothing applies without accept or reject. Bring your own Anthropic/OpenAI key, or use ours
- **Two products, one platform** — **Plot** for screenwriting (Fountain-native, Final Draft FDX import/export); **Canvas** for general structured documents (coming soon)

Free plan available; paid plans from $19/mo. Details: [pricing](https://inkwell.software/pricing).
